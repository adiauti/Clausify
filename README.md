# Clausify

**AI-powered legal document analysis. Know what you're signing — before you sign it.**

---

## What is Clausify?

Clausify helps individuals and small businesses understand legal documents without needing a lawyer. Most people sign contracts — employment agreements, NDAs, rental leases, freelance contracts — without fully understanding what they're agreeing to. Clausify changes that.

Upload any contract and within seconds you get a plain-English summary, colour-coded risk flags highlighted directly inside the document, and a live AI chat assistant that answers your specific questions about it.

---

## Features

- **Plain-English summary** — understand the document at a glance, no legal jargon
- **Inline risk highlighting** — high, medium, and low risk clauses colour-coded directly in the document text
- **Risk score** — an overall risk rating with a breakdown of flagged clauses
- **AI chat assistant** — ask anything about the document and get a clear, direct answer
- **Secure accounts** — sign in with email or Google; documents saved to your account for future reference
- **Multiple formats** — supports PDF, JPEG, PNG, and plain text input
